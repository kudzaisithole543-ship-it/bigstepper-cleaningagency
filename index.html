Our Cleaning Services

BigStepper Cleaning Agency provides reliable, affordable, and professional cleaning services for homes, businesses, and commercial properties. We are committed to leaving every space sparkling clean.

Residential Cleaning

Professional cleaning for apartments, houses, and residential properties, including regular maintenance, one-time cleans, and scheduled cleaning services to keep your home fresh and spotless.

Commercial Cleaning

Reliable cleaning for offices, retail shops, warehouses, factories, schools, and commercial buildings, creating a clean and productive working environment.

Deep & Spring Cleaning

Intensive cleaning for kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, and workspaces, removing hidden dirt, dust, and grime with attention to every detail.

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Thorough cleaning before moving into a new property or after vacating, ensuring the space is fresh, hygienic, and ready for its next occupant.

Specialized & Dry Cleaning

We provide expert carpet cleaning, window cleaning, sanitization, post-construction cleaning, and professional dry cleaning services for suits, dresses, uniforms, curtains, blankets, and other delicate garments through trusted cleaning partners.

Important Notice: Our dry cleaning services are offered through carefully selected professional partners, ensuring your garments receive high-quality care and professional handling.

Car Wash & Detailing

Complete vehicle care including exterior washing, interior valet, premium detailing, engine bay cleaning, waxing, polishing, leather treatment, and paint protection.
